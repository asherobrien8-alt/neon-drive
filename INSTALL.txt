NEON DRIVE for the Snowsky Echo Mini — install
================================================

BEFORE YOU START
 1. Copy any music on the player's INTERNAL storage to your PC (installing can wipe it).
 2. Download the official FiiO Echo Mini firmware and keep the zip somewhere safe.
    It's your undo button: https://fiio.com/newsinfo/1144405.html
 3. Take the microSD card OUT of the player.
 4. Charge the player above 50%.

STEP 1 - FIRMWARE
 1. Plug the Echo Mini into your PC. It shows up as a drive.
 2. Open the STEP-1-FIRMWARE folder and copy HIFIEC90.IMG onto the player's drive,
    at the very top level (NOT inside any folder).
 3. In Windows, right-click the player's drive and choose Eject. Unplug it.
 4. Restart the player. It installs by itself. Wait until it boots up.
 5. Check it took: SYSTEM -> Firmware must say "Neon Drive 5".
    Do STEP 1 again for every new version - the theme alone is not enough.

STEP 2 - THEME
 1. Plug the player back in. The screen says "USB mass storage" and it shows up as a drive.
 2. Open the STEP-2-THEME folder. Copy the ".rechord" folder onto the top level
    of the player's drive. If Windows asks, click "Replace the files".
 3. Eject, unplug. The theme loads on the next start.

STEP 3 - FIRST START
 1. The first start builds the music library by itself ("UPDATING LIBRARY").
    It does it again every time you copy music over USB and unplug.
 2. Put the microSD card back in. If you put new music on the card with a
    card reader: MUSIC -> Organize -> Rebuild library.

CONTROLS
 VOL + / -        move up / down, change volume on Now Playing
 PLAY             open / select
 hold PLAY        A-Z jump in any long list (artists, albums, songs)
 BACK             go back (the full menu is under SYSTEM)
 hold VOL + / -   on Now Playing: next / previous song
 MENU             quick settings (shuffle, repeat, cover art, sleep timer)
 hold POWER       switch on (a quick tap does nothing) / power-off animation, then off
 Search           MUSIC -> Search. VOL picks a letter, PLAY adds it,
                  BACK deletes, hold PLAY for the results.

PLAYLISTS
 Put .m3u / .m3u8 playlist files in a folder called "Playlists" at the top of
 the player. Songs stay in Music (one copy each); the playlist just points at
 them. Copied over USB they appear by themselves. They show up in
 MUSIC -> Playlists, together with Favorites and every music folder on the
 SD card (each folder is its own playlist automatically).
 Hold PLAY on a song inside an album or playlist to add it to Favorites.

COPYING MUSIC OVER USB
 Copy music to the player's own storage ("Internal"). Over USB the SD card
 is READ-ONLY (that is the "remove the write protection" message): to put
 music on the SD card, use a card reader in the PC.

COVER ART
 Run cover-art-maker\Make Cover Art.bat once with the player plugged in
 (needs Python 3), then on the player: MENU -> Cover art -> on.

PLAYS: FLAC, MP3, WAV.  NOT YET: M4A/AAC, OGG, APE, DSD.
Bluetooth headphones work too (still early).

GOING BACK TO NORMAL
 Copy the official FiiO HIFIECxx.IMG (from step 2 of "Before you start") onto the
 top level of the player, eject, restart. Done - it's stock again.
 If the player ever won't turn on at all, don't panic: follow the "Recovery" section at
 https://github.com/brnomt/FiioDcomp/blob/master/docs/FLASHING.md
