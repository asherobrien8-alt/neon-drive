NEON DRIVE for the Snowsky Echo Mini — install
================================================

BEFORE YOU START
 1. Copy any music on the player's INTERNAL storage to your PC (installing can wipe it).
 2. Download the official FiiO Echo Mini firmware and keep the zip somewhere safe.
    It's your undo button: https://fiio.com/newsinfo/1144405.html
 3. Take the microSD card OUT of the player.
 4. Charge the player above 50%.

STEP 1 - FIRMWARE
 1. Plug the Echo Mini into your PC. It shows up as a drive.
 2. Open the STEP-1-FIRMWARE folder and copy HIFIEC90.IMG onto the player's drive,
    at the very top level (NOT inside any folder).
 3. In Windows, right-click the player's drive and choose Eject. Unplug it.
 4. Restart the player. It installs by itself. Wait until it boots up.

STEP 2 - THEME
 1. Plug the player back in. The screen says "USB mass storage" and it shows up as a drive.
 2. Open the STEP-2-THEME folder. Copy the ".rechord" folder onto the top level
    of the player's drive. If Windows asks, click "Replace the files".
 3. Eject, unplug. The theme loads on the next start.

STEP 3 - FIRST START
 1. On the home screen go to SYSTEM SETTINGS -> Rebuild index.
    (Do this again whenever you add new music.)
 2. Put the microSD card back in.

CONTROLS
 VOL + / -        move up / down, change volume on Now Playing
 PLAY             open / select
 hold PLAY        A-Z jump in any long list (artists, albums, songs)
 BACK             go back (on the home screen: the full menu)
 MENU             quick settings (shuffle, repeat, playlist)
 Search           MEDIA LIBRARY -> Search. VOL picks a letter, PLAY adds it,
                  BACK deletes, hold PLAY for the results.

PLAYS: FLAC, MP3, WAV.  NOT YET: M4A/AAC, OGG, APE, DSD, Bluetooth, USB DAC.

GOING BACK TO NORMAL
 Copy the official FiiO HIFIECxx.IMG (from step 2 of "Before you start") onto the
 top level of the player, eject, restart. Done - it's stock again.
 If the player ever won't turn on at all, don't panic: follow the "Recovery" section at
 https://github.com/brnomt/FiioDcomp/blob/master/docs/FLASHING.md
