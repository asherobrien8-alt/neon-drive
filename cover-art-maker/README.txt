Cover art for Neon Drive
========================
The player cannot decode JPEGs itself, so covers are prepared on the PC once:

1. Plug in the Echo Mini (and the SD card in a card reader if you use one).
2. Double-click "Make Cover Art.bat" and type the drive letters.
   It reads the covers embedded in your songs (or cover.jpg / folder.jpg in
   the album folder) and writes .rechord\ARTPACK.BIN on the player.
3. Eject. On the player press MENU (quick settings) > Cover art > on.

Run it again after adding music. Needs Python 3 from python.org.
artpack.py is ReChord's tool (GPL-3.0), run with 80 px covers for this theme.
